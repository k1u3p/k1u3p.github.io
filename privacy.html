Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

"OpenClaw and OpenMausBot personal assistant" is a private application operated by one individual for their own use. The only Google account that uses it is the owner's own.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it accesses the owner's Gmail messages and settings and Google Calendar events, in order to read, search, draft, organise and (with the owner's per-message approval) send email, and to read and manage calendar entries.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests. The application's access token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train generalised AI models. Content read from Gmail or Calendar is processed by the owner's AI assistant to answer the owner's requests and is not made available to anyone else.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the application's stored token ends all access.
